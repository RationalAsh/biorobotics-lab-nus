Guangshan Wang obtained his B.E. degree in Robotics Engineering from the Department of Mechanical and Energy Engineering at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), Shenzhen, China, in 2026. During his undergraduate studies, his research focused on hip-disarticulation prosthetic devices and resistive exoskeletons. 

He is currently a Ph.D. student in the Department of Biomedical Engineering at the National University of Singapore, where he is supervised by Dr. Yu Haoyong. His current research focuses on wearable robotics.
